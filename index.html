Atlas

Atlas is a personal assistant used by one household. It is not a public service.

Privacy policy

Atlas reads Gmail and Google Calendar with read-only access, only for the account owner. It finds actions in that data (bills, school tasks, bookings) and creates reminders. All data is processed and stored on the owner's own computer and phone, using a local AI model. No data is sent to third parties, sold or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
